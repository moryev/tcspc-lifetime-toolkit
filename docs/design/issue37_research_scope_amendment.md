# Proposed Issue #37 research-use scope amendment

Pending maintainer review and publication to the issue.

## Revised closure objective

Deliver K=3 Poisson IRF reconvolution as an explicitly experimental, exploratory
research feature with caller-selected settings, usable measurement integration,
transparent diagnostics and reproducible teaching examples.

## Delivered under the revised scope

- Module-qualified three-component reconvolution with exact-zero components,
  equal lifetimes, bounded shared shift and one background.
- Numerically safeguarded TRF and L-BFGS-B implementations, finite-candidate
  retention, explicit budgets, candidate-specific validation and transparent
  conditioning diagnostics.
- Canonical raw-measurement integration with IRF selection, normalization history
  and measurement/source provenance.
- One reproducible separated/coincident-component research example using explicit
  TRF settings, expected-count curves, residuals and parameter diagnostics.
- Focused regression coverage and documented scientific limitations.

## Deferred work

- General production-policy qualification and resource-allocation studies.
- Universal independent-audit completion or agreement.
- Large-scale frozen scientific qualification.
- Guaranteed identification or accurate recovery of every individual component.

## Retained evidence and closure gate

The audit-only verification produced six finite, freshly assessed and locally
validated candidates, while strict all-starts-complete acceptance failed at 0/6.
The historical audit outcome and original qualification targets remain recorded.
The original production-policy and scientific-qualification criteria remain
unachieved and deferred under this amendment.

Experimental closure requires review and merge of the focused measurement,
example and documentation checkpoint, passing applicable regressions/package
checks, and explicit maintainer acceptance of this revised scope. Individual
lifetimes remain model-conditional candidate estimates. Numerical acceptance,
curve accuracy and physical identifiability must be reported separately.

Follow-up production or qualification work requires its own reviewed scope and
authorization. Existing mono/bi scientific records, the root API, persistence
schema and published release metadata retain their established contracts.
